Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Sentinel Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Charging Animation - 3D Fun FX mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

To provide its features, the App reads information from your device. This information is processed locally and is never sent to us:

  • Battery status, such as charge level, temperature, health, voltage, technology, and charging state
  • Your app preferences, such as your selected and favorite animations and Charge Alarm settings

3. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use device information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general usage data, to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information in advertising

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings under Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Permissions

The App may request permissions that are needed for its features, such as showing animations while your phone is charging and sending Charge Alarm notifications. These permissions are used only to provide those features, and you can change them at any time in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information, no personal data is stored on our servers. Information the App uses stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
